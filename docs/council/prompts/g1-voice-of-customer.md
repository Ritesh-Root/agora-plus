# Your task: Voice-of-customer gap finder (prefix VOC)
Write your report to `docs/council/reports/g1-voice-of-customer.md`. Read files, use read-only git, and the web via Cloudflare only.

We already researched Alexa+ complaints in Phase 0b: `docs/research/alexa-plus-voice-of-customer.md` and
`docs/research/feature-matrix.md`. Your job is to find what is NEW or MISSED since then, and what AGORA+ still does not answer.

1. Scrape fresh complaints about Alexa+ (Aug-Oct 2026): Reddit r/alexa, r/amazonecho, r/smarthome, r/homeassistant;
   Hacker News; tech press (The Verge, Wired, Ars Technica, CNET, Tom's Guide); app store review summaries if reachable.
2. Scrape what people say they want from an agentic home assistant or AI family assistant (memory, proactivity, shared family
   use, approvals, privacy, integrations). Include people who switched from Alexa to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and why.
3. Build a table: pain point or want, how often you saw it (count of distinct sources), best quote + URL, already in our
   research? (yes/no), what AGORA+ does about it today (file evidence), gap: none / partial / missing.
4. Findings = the gaps. For each, propose the smallest AGORA+ feature that answers it and can be built and demoed before
   2026-10-23, naming the MCP tool or UI it would touch. Flag gaps that would weaken our pitch if a judge asked about them.
